Page intro:

> You don't need to sign up or log in to use this site. We only process what the site needs to run and to handle your feedback, and we don't sell personal information.

## 1. Data stored in your browser

Saved items, read history, light or dark appearance, recent searches (up to 10), whether you've seen the latest changelog, and feedback drafts are kept in your current browser. Your place in a list, scroll position and expanded sections are kept only in the current tab's session storage and are cleared when you close the tab. None of this is sent to the server. Clearing your browser data removes it, and it doesn't sync across devices. Saved items, read history and appearance can be exported and imported on the "Saved" page.

## 2. Feedback

What you submit on the feedback page, the email you choose to leave, the address of the page you were on, and any screenshot you attach are stored on the server to handle your feedback. To prevent abuse, the server stores an identifier computed from your network address and browser type that cannot be reversed, used for rate limiting and blocking abusive sources.

## 3. Server and network logs

By default this software does not collect visitor analytics. Your server, reverse proxy or CDN may keep access logs under its own settings (such as IP address, time, page address and browser information). State here the services you actually use and how long logs are kept.

## 4. Third-party content

This site shows summaries of and links to third-party articles. When you follow a link to the original, you are on that site, and its privacy policy applies.
